Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

1. Introduction

Vibely ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Vibely LinkedIn Extractor Chrome extension. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our extension.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-in: Email and profile information when you sign in with Google to access Pro features.
  • Payment Data: We use Razorpay for payments. We do not store your card details; Razorpay handles all payment processing securely.
  • LinkedIn Cookies: The extension requires access to LinkedIn cookies to extract profile data on your behalf when you perform searches.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and manage subscriptions
  • To process payments and deliver Pro features
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our extension and services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Razorpay: For payment processing
  • Google: For authentication (OAuth)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. Payment data is processed by Razorpay's secure infrastructure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at the email below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.
